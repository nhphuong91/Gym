<text font-size="12" font-weight="500" fill="#8b5e00" x="50" y="139">⚠ What rolling does — and what it can't</text>
<text font-size="11" fill="#9e6e10" x="50" y="156">Rolling eases the feeling of tightness, mostly through your nervous system: pain and tension settle</text>
<text font-size="11" fill="#9e6e10" x="50" y="169">for a while. It doesn't 'release' fascia, and harder pressure adds nothing. Light pressure, muscle</text>
<text font-size="11" fill="#9e6e10" x="50" y="182">only — EDS tissue needs care. Tightness is what rolling is for; range is not (R7).</text>

<text font-size="11" fill="#444" x="64" y="913">Muscle only — never on a joint, tendon, bony point, the kneecap or the back of the knee</text>
<rect x="50" y="925" width="7" height="7" rx="1" fill="#e8a020"/>
<text font-size="11" fill="#444" x="64" y="933">Light pressure: about 3–4/10, never sharp — harder adds nothing; lacrosse ball: same limit, lean in less</text>
<rect x="50" y="945" width="7" height="7" rx="1" fill="#e8a020"/>
<text font-size="11" fill="#444" x="64" y="953">Slow passes, 2–4 s each way; pause up to 20–30 s on a tender spot; 30–60 s an area</text>
<rect x="50" y="965" width="7" height="7" rx="1" fill="#e8a020"/>
<text font-size="11" fill="#444" x="64" y="973">Hands holding you up: on fists, elbows soft (R2); trunk braced when face down (R3)</text>
<rect x="50" y="985" width="7" height="7" rx="1" fill="#e8a020"/>
<text font-size="11" fill="#444" x="64" y="993">Positions stay short of end range: ankle over knee or knee out only as far as comfortable (R1)</text>

<text font-size="11" fill="#444" x="64" y="1013">Tingling, numbness, or a joint that feels looser → stop and move off; leave that area out next time (§2.6)</text>
