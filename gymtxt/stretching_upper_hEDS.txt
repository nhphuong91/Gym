<!-- hEDS PHILOSOPHY -->

<text font-size="12" font-weight="500" fill="#8b5e00" x="50" y="139">⚠ Why no passive stretching — hEDS / HSD principle</text>
<text font-size="11" fill="#9e6e10" x="50" y="156">Your connective tissue already has excessive laxity. Passive stretching bypasses muscles and</text>
<text font-size="11" fill="#9e6e10" x="50" y="169">loads ligaments/joint capsules instead → more instability. Every drill below keeps muscles ACTIVE</text>
<text font-size="11" fill="#9e6e10" x="50" y="182">throughout the entire range so joints stay protected while tissue quality improves.</text>
